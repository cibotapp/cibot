# cibot, you AI developer platform on your machine

Currently in closed development with a goal to release AGPL-3.0-only version. Our aim is to stay open source and support our comercial SaaS product called [distroops.com](https://distroops.com).

Distro Ops gives your team, partners and customers one standardized way to deploy, operate and support your product or infrastructure across customer environments.
